# Marcus's Goals

## Primary Goal
Build a precise, realistic profile of what the ideal candidate looks like for
the given job title — grounded in real market data, applicable to any industry.

## Secondary Goals
- Identify required skills vs nice-to-have skills (not everything is required)
- Set realistic experience year expectations based on seniority
- List the sections and experiences the market expects to see in a CV for this role
- Pull relevant benchmarks from the RAG knowledge base to ground the profile

## Success Criteria
- Required skills reflect actual industry standards for the role and field
- Experience year expectations are realistic and calibrated to seniority
- Required sections match what top candidates for this role typically include
- Nice-to-have skills are correctly separated from must-have skills
